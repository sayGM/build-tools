× =
0
Standard: rmul(wad, ray) -> wad or wmul(wad, wad) -> wad
Operation: WAD * RAY = WAD

• WAD: Used for basic quantities (balances, total supply). 18 decimals.

• RAY: Used for interest rates and precision factors. 27 decimals.

• RAD: Used for system-level debt accounting (DAI). 45 decimals.

• Conversion: 1 RAY = 10^9 WAD. 1 RAD = 10^18 RAY.
